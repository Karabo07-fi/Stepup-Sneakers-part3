About StepUp Sneakers

Our Story

Founded in 2024 in Bloemfontein, StepUp Sneakers started as a passion project for sneaker heads. We bring authentic branded footwear and streetwear to the Free State.

Mission

To provide authentic, trendy sneakers and excellent customer service to sneaker lovers across South Africa.

Vision

To become the go-to sneaker destination for youth and collectors in the Free State.
